## Purpose and Scope

 This document describes the reranking model subsystem in Xinference. Reranking models are specialized neural networks that score and re-order a list of documents based on their relevance to a query. They are commonly used in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines to improve the quality of retrieved documents before passing them to LLMs.

 For information about other model types, see [Model Types](https://deepwiki.com/xorbitsai/inference/4-model-types). For details on launching and managing models, see [Model Management Endpoints](https://deepwiki.com/xorbitsai/inference/5.1-model-management-endpoints).

 **Sources:** [xinference/model/rerank/core.py1-226](https://github.com/xorbitsai/inference/blob/d97e0970/xinference/model/rerank/core.py#L1-L226)

### Normal vs LLM-based Rerankers

 
 - **Normal**: Typically uses cross-encoders where the query and document are concatenated and passed through a transformer (e.g., XLM-RoBERTa).
 - **LLM-based**: Uses the hidden states or logprobs of a decoder-only model (like Gemma) to determine relevance [xinference/model/rerank/core.py193-197](https://github.com/xorbitsai/inference/blob/d97e0970/xinference/model/rerank/core.py#L193-L197)

## Purpose and Scope

 This document provides detailed information about DeepSeek-R1, a large language model developed to enhance reasoning capabilities through a combination of supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL). This page focuses specifically on the DeepSeek-R1 model architecture and its training methodology, which employs a cold-start approach followed by reinforcement learning. For information about DeepSeek-R1-Zero (which uses pure RL without SFT), see [DeepSeek-R1-Zero](https://deepwiki.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1/2.1-deepseek-r1-zero), and for details on the distilled models derived from DeepSeek-R1, see [Distilled Models](https://deepwiki.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1/2.3-distilled-models).

## Model Architecture

 DeepSeek-R1 is based on the DeepSeek-V3-Base architecture, employing a Mixture of Experts (MoE) design. The model has the following key specifications:

 
 - **Total Parameters**: 671 billion
 - **Activated Parameters**: 37 billion
 - **Context Length**: 128K tokens
 
 The model uses sparse activation via the MoE architecture, allowing it to maintain a large parameter count while only activating a fraction of parameters during inference, balancing computational efficiency with model capacity.

## Training Methodology

 DeepSeek-R1 follows a distinctive training approach that addresses limitations observed in the DeepSeek-R1-Zero model (such as endless repetition, poor readability, and language mixing). The training pipeline incorporates both supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL) stages.

### Training Stages

 
 - **SFT Stage 1 (Cold-start)**: This initial supervised fine-tuning phase serves as the seed for the model's reasoning capabilities, providing foundational knowledge and skills.
 - **SFT Stage 2**: This phase enhances the model's non-reasoning capabilities, ensuring balanced performance across various tasks.
 - **RL Stage 1**: The first reinforcement learning stage focuses on discovering improved reasoning patterns, optimizing the model's problem-solving approaches.
 - **RL Stage 2**: The second RL stage aims to align the model's outputs with human preferences, ensuring its responses are useful, readable, and appropriate.
 
 This combined approach results in a model that maintains the strong reasoning capabilities derived from reinforcement learning while addressing the usability issues found in models trained exclusively through RL.

### Emergent Capabilities

 DeepSeek-R1 exhibits several emergent reasoning behaviors, refined through its training process:

 
 - **Chain-of-Thought Reasoning**: The model naturally develops step-by-step reasoning approaches to solve complex problems.
 - **Self-verification**: The ability to check its own work and correct mistakes.
 - **Reflection**: The capacity to evaluate the quality of its own reasoning.

## Usage Guidelines

 To achieve optimal performance with DeepSeek-R1, certain usage configurations are recommended:

 
### Recommended Settings

 
 - **Temperature**: Set to 0.6 (acceptable range: 0.5-0.7) to prevent endless repetitions or incoherent outputs.
 - **No System Prompts**: All instructions should be contained within the user prompt.
 - **Mathematical Problem Format**: Include directives like "Please reason step by step, and put your final answer within \boxed{}."
 - **Thinking Pattern**: Enforce the model to start its response with "<think>\n" to ensure thorough reasoning.

### Key Differences from DeepSeek-R1-Zero

 The primary distinction between DeepSeek-R1 and DeepSeek-R1-Zero lies in their training methodologies:

 
 - **DeepSeek-R1-Zero**: Trained purely through reinforcement learning without SFT, which resulted in emergent reasoning capabilities but also led to issues like endless repetition, poor readability, and language mixing.
 - **DeepSeek-R1**: Incorporates a cold-start approach with supervised fine-tuning before applying reinforcement learning, maintaining strong reasoning capabilities while addressing the usability issues of DeepSeek-R1-Zero.

## Knowledge Distillation

 DeepSeek-R1 serves as the source model for knowledge distillation to create smaller, more accessible models that retain its reasoning capabilities. This process involves generating high-quality reasoning data with DeepSeek-R1 and using it to fine-tune smaller models based on Qwen and Llama architectures.

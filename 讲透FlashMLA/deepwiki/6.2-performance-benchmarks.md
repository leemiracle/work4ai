## Performance Metrics

### Primary Metrics

 
 - **Execution Time**: Wall-clock time in milliseconds.
 - **FLOPS (Floating Point Operations Per Second)**: Theoretical compute throughput in TFLOPS.
 - **Memory Bandwidth**: Effective memory throughput in GB/s.

## Performance Analysis Guidelines

 
### Workload Classification

 The benchmarking results can be analyzed using the FLOPs-to-bytes ratio to understand workload characteristics:

 
 - **Compute-Bound**: High FLOPS/byte ratio, benefits from tensor core optimization.
 - **Memory-Bound**: Low FLOPS/byte ratio, limited by memory bandwidth.
 
 
### Optimization Targets

 Based on hardware specifications for SM90 (Hopper):

 
 - **Compute Target**: Effective utilization of H800's peak TFLOPS.
 - **Memory Target**: Effective utilization of H800's HBM3 bandwidth (up to 3.35 TB/s).

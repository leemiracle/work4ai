## 1. Overview and Resource Types

 WeKnora allows users to share two primary resource types with collaborative organizations:

 
 - **Knowledge Bases**: Handled via `KBShareService`, supporting configurable read or write permissions (viewer, editor, admin).
 - **Agents**: Handled via `agentShareService`, always restricted to read-only (`viewer`) permission for organization members [internal/application/service/agent_share.go149-187](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/agent_share.go#L149-L187)
 
 A share associates a resource owned by a specific tenant with an organization. All members of that organization gain access to the resource subject to their organization role and tenant RBAC caps. Sharing requires the caller to hold at least the `editor` role inside the target organization [internal/application/service/kbshare.go78-148](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/kbshare.go#L78-L148)

## 3. Permission Resolution

 WeKnora implements a "Plan 3" tenancy architecture where membership is keyed on tenants (`organization_tenant_members`) rather than individual user IDs [internal/application/service/organization.go41-45](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/organization.go#L41-L45)

 Effective permissions are calculated using a three-dimensional constraint:

 
```
effective = min(share.Permission, tenant_org_role, tenant_role_cap)
```

 
 - **`share.Permission`**: The grant level assigned by the resource owner [internal/application/service/kbshare.go30](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/kbshare.go#L30-L30)
 - **`tenant_org_role`**: The role of the caller’s tenant within the target organization [internal/application/service/kbshare.go30](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/kbshare.go#L30-L30)
 - **`tenant_role_cap`**: The system ceiling ensuring a tenant cannot exceed its local authorization tier [internal/application/service/kbshare.go68-73](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/kbshare.go#L68-L73)

## 4. Knowledge Base Sharing

 
### Service Implementation — `KBShareService`

 The `KBShareService` coordinates sharing operations. Key methods include:

 
 - `ShareKnowledgeBase`: Validates ownership and organization membership, then persists the share record [internal/application/service/kbshare.go78-148](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/kbshare.go#L78-L148)
 - `UpdateSharePermission`: Modifies permissions on existing shares [internal/application/service/kbshare.go161-188](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/kbshare.go#L161-L188)
 - `RemoveShare`: Revokes access by deleting the share entity [internal/application/service/kbshare.go192-218](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/kbshare.go#L192-L218)

## 5. Agent Sharing

 
### Service Implementation — `agentShareService`

 Managed via `agentShareService` in `internal/application/service/agent_share.go`:

 
 - **Pre-share Validation**: The `ShareAgent` method verifies that the agent has a valid `Config.ModelID`. If `knowledge_search` is enabled, a `Config.RerankModelID` must also be present [internal/application/service/agent_share.go40-57](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/agent_share.go#L40-L57)
 - **Read-Only Enforcement**: Agents are shared exclusively with `OrgRoleViewer` privilege [internal/application/service/agent_share.go149-187](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/agent_share.go#L149-L187)

  This document describes the organization management system in WeKnora, which enables collaborative workspaces for sharing knowledge bases and agents across multiple users and tenants. Organizations provide a structured permission model for controlled resource sharing and team collaboration.

 **Scope:** This page covers organization creation, tenant members, invitations, role assignment, and the multi-tenant role-based access control (RBAC) permission model. For details on the sharing of resources like knowledge bases and agents, see **Resource Sharing (12.2)**.

 
---

 
## Conceptual Model

 
### Organizations as Collaboration Spaces

 Organizations in WeKnora function as shared collaboration units that facilitate cross-tenant teamwork and sharing. Following the architectural "Plan 3" ([issue #1303](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/issue #1303)), membership is managed at the **tenant level** instead of individual users. Each member is a tenant (a workspace or organization under a user account) — users serve as representatives for UI and audit purposes, but **permissions are decided per tenant** [internal/application/service/organization.go40-46](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/organization.go#L40-L46) [internal/types/organization.go109-114](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/types/organization.go#L109-L114)

## Access Control Model

 
### Role Hierarchy and Effective Permission

 WeKnora employs a strict hierarchical Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) model in organizations with three roles assigned per-tenant:

 
| Role | Constant | Permission Level | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admin | OrgRoleAdmin | 3 | Full org control and member management |
| Editor | OrgRoleEditor | 2 | Can edit shared knowledge bases but not manage org settings |
| Viewer | OrgRoleViewer | 1 | Read-only access to shared resources |

 Role validity and hierarchy checking are implemented via the `OrgMemberRole` type and its methods like `IsValid` and `HasPermission` [internal/types/organization.go9-39](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/types/organization.go#L9-L39)

 
### 3-Dimension Permission Cap

 The **effective permission** for accessing a shared resource is computed as:

 
```
effective_permission = min(
    share_grant,
    tenant_org_role,
    user_tenant_role_cap
)
```

 
 - **share_grant**: Permission granted on the resource share (viewer/editor/admin)
 - **tenant_org_role**: Role of the user's tenant in the organization
 - **user_tenant_role_cap**: Tenant role cap limits users with role `Viewer` in their tenant to no more than `Viewer` permission on shares (enforced to avoid privilege escalation)
 
 This logic is encapsulated in `applyTenantRoleCap` and related methods in the KB share service [internal/application/service/kbshare.go65-74](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/kbshare.go#L65-L74)

## Member Management and Invitations

 
### Organization Creation

 Organizations are created via `organizationService.CreateOrganization`. The creating user's tenant is automatically added as an `Admin` member. Invite codes are generated and have configurable validity periods (0, 1, 7, 30 days). Organizations have a configurable member limit (default to 200 tenants, 0 means unlimited) [internal/application/service/organization.go77-146](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/internal/application/service/organization.go#L77-L146)

 
### Joining Organizations

 Tenants join organizations either directly via invite codes or by approval-based join requests if the org has `RequireApproval = true`. Upon joining, all users of the tenant gain access to that organization's shared resources according to their roles.

## Frontend Implementation

 
### Organization Settings Modal

 The Vue component `OrganizationSettingsModal.vue` provides an interface for organization admins to:

 
 - Edit basic org information (name, description, avatar with emoji picker)
 - View and copy invite codes
 - See RBAC hints if their tenant role limits actions
 - Invite members (if admin) and view pending invitations
 
 The modal maintains sections for basic info, permissions explanation, and invitation management [frontend/src/views/organization/OrganizationSettingsModal.vue47-123](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/frontend/src/views/organization/OrganizationSettingsModal.vue#L47-L123)

 
### Tenant Members Management

 Tenant members (users inside a tenant) are managed inside `TenantMembers.vue`, showing:

 
 - A list of members within a tenant, their roles, and avatars
 - A role permission matrix popover explaining roles and capabilities
 - Pending invitations with management actions (revoke, resend)
 - Audit log visibility for tenant owners and admins
 
 This component tightly integrates with the tenant member service APIs and guides member lifecycle management [frontend/src/views/settings/TenantMembers.vue1-137](https://github.com/Tencent/WeKnora/blob/10973ed3/frontend/src/views/settings/TenantMembers.vue#L1-L137)

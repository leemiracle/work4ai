## Content Update and Maintenance Patterns

 The repository structure facilitates easy maintenance and content updates through consistent organizational patterns:

 
 - **Model Tables**: Standardized format across all sections enables easy addition of new models
 - **Size-based Organization**: Consistent grouping by parameter count (1.3B, 5.7B, 6.7B, 33B)
 - **Format Variants**: Systematic coverage of base/instruct and quantization formats
 - **Bilingual Synchronization**: Parallel structure between English and Chinese versions
 - **External Link Management**: Centralized reference to official platforms reduces maintenance overhead
 
 The documentation serves as an information multiplier, directing users to appropriate resources within the broader DeepSeek Coder ecosystem while maintaining a lightweight, easily navigable structure.

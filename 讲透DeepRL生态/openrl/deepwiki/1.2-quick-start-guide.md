  This guide provides a quick introduction to OpenRL, a unified reinforcement learning framework that supports various training tasks including single-agent, multi-agent, offline RL, self-play, and natural language processing. It covers installation, basic usage, and a simple example to get you started with reinforcement learning using OpenRL.

 For detailed information on specific features, please refer to other relevant wiki pages such as:

 
 - For multi-agent training, see [Multi-Agent System](https://deepwiki.com/OpenRL-Lab/openrl/3-agents-and-algorithms)

 - For customizing environments, see [Custom Environments](https://deepwiki.com/OpenRL-Lab/openrl/2.3-custom-environments)

## Next Steps

 After mastering the basics, you can explore more advanced features:

 
 - **Multi-agent training**: OpenRL supports training multiple agents in cooperative or competitive settings.
 - **Natural language processing**: Train language models using reinforcement learning.
 - **Custom environments**: Integrate your own environments into OpenRL.
 - **Advanced configuration**: Customize training parameters, callbacks, and logging.
 - **Algorithms**: Explore different algorithms like PPO, DQN, VDN, SAC, and more.
 
 For detailed guides on these topics, refer to the corresponding wiki pages or the official [documentation](https://openrl-docs.readthedocs.io/).

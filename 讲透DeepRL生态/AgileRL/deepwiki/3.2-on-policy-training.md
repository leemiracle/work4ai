  This document covers AgileRL's on-policy training framework, specifically the implementation and usage of the `train_on_policy` function and its integration with evolutionary hyperparameter optimization. On-policy training involves learning from experiences collected by the current policy without storing them in a replay buffer. For off-policy training that uses experience replay, see [Off-Policy Training](https://deepwiki.com/AgileRL/AgileRL/3.1-off-policy-training). For offline training from static datasets, see [Offline Training](https://deepwiki.com/AgileRL/AgileRL/3.3-offline-training).

 
## Overview

 On-policy reinforcement learning algorithms learn directly from experiences generated by the current policy being trained. Unlike off-policy methods, these algorithms do not reuse past experiences from different policies, making them more stable but potentially less sample efficient. The primary on-policy algorithm implemented in AgileRL is PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization).

 The on-policy training system in AgileRL combines policy gradient methods with evolutionary hyperparameter optimization, allowing populations of agents to evolve their hyperparameters and network architectures while learning from their current policy experiences.

## Key Differences from Off-Policy Training

 On-policy training differs fundamentally from off-policy methods in several important ways:

 
| Aspect | On-Policy | Off-Policy |
|---|---|---|
| Experience Storage | Temporary, discarded after use | Persistent replay buffer |
| Learning Frequency | After collecting batch | Continuously from replay buffer |
| Sample Efficiency | Lower (no reuse) | Higher (experience reuse) |
| Stability | Higher (current policy data) | Lower (mixed policy data) |
| Memory Requirements | Minimal | Significant (replay buffer) |

 The absence of a replay buffer in on-policy training means experiences are collected, used once for learning, and then discarded. This approach provides stability guarantees but requires more environment interactions.

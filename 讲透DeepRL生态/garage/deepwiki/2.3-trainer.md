  The Trainer is a core component of the Garage reinforcement learning framework that orchestrates the training process. It manages the lifecycle of experiments, coordinates interactions between algorithms and environments, and provides utilities for tracking metrics, logging, and saving snapshots for experiment resumption.

 For information about specific experiment setup and decorators, see [Experiment System](https://deepwiki.com/rlworkgroup/garage/2.2-experiment-system).

 
## Purpose and Core Functionality

 The Trainer acts as the central coordinator for reinforcement learning experiments in Garage. Its primary responsibilities include:

 
 - Setting up algorithms and environments
 - Managing the training loop and epoch iterations
 - Collecting samples from environments using samplers
 - Tracking metrics and logging experiment progress
 - Handling experiment snapshots for saving and resuming training

## Summary

 The Trainer is a pivotal component in the Garage framework that orchestrates the reinforcement learning training process. It abstracts away the complexity of coordinating algorithms, environments, samplers, and policies, allowing users to focus on their experiment design rather than implementation details. With features like snapshotting and resumption, it facilitates the development and refinement of reinforcement learning agents.

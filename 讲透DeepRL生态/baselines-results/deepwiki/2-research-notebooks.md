## Multi-Algorithm Comparison Study

 The primary notebook `acktr_ppo_acer_a2c_atari.ipynb` conducts comprehensive performance analysis across four reinforcement learning algorithms on the Atari benchmark suite.

 
### Algorithm Coverage

 The notebook analyzes the following algorithms:

 
 - **A2C** (Advantage Actor-Critic): Synchronous policy gradient method
 - **ACER** (Actor-Critic with Experience Replay): Off-policy actor-critic with replay buffer
 - **ACKTR** (Actor-Critic using Kronecker-Factored Trust Region): Natural policy gradient with K-FAC optimization
 - **PPO** (Proximal Policy Optimization): Clipped policy gradient method

## Analysis and Visualization Patterns

 The notebooks generate standardized performance comparisons and learning curves across algorithms and environments. The analysis focuses on:

 
 - **Episode Rewards**: Cumulative reward progression during training
 - **Learning Curves**: Performance improvement over training steps
 - **Sample Efficiency**: Reward per environment interaction
 - **Final Performance**: Steady-state policy performance
 
 The visualization pipeline uses `matplotlib` for generating publication-quality figures with consistent styling and formatting across all experiments.

## Purpose and Scope

 This document covers the comprehensive multi-algorithm comparison study implemented in the primary analysis notebook of the baselines-results repository. The study evaluates four state-of-the-art policy gradient algorithms—ACKTR, PPO, ACER, and A2C—across the Atari 2600 game benchmark suite.

 This analysis serves as the flagship research content of the repository, providing empirical comparisons of algorithm performance, training efficiency, and convergence characteristics. For individual algorithm deep-dives, see [ACKTR Deep Dive](https://deepwiki.com/openai/baselines-results/2.2-acktr-deep-dive) and [DQN Analysis](https://deepwiki.com/openai/baselines-results/2.3-dqn-analysis). ## Algorithm Portfolio

 The comparison study evaluates four distinct reinforcement learning algorithms, each representing different approaches to policy optimization and variance reduction:

 
| Algorithm | Full Name | Key Innovation | Algorithm Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACKTR | Actor-Critic using Kronecker-Factored Trust Region | Natural gradients with K-FAC approximation | Policy Gradient + Trust Region |
| PPO | Proximal Policy Optimization | Clipped surrogate objective | Policy Gradient + Trust Region |
| ACER | Actor-Critic with Experience Replay | Off-policy corrections for experience replay | Policy Gradient + Experience Replay |
| A2C | Advantage Actor-Critic | Synchronous advantage estimation | Policy Gradient + Value Function |

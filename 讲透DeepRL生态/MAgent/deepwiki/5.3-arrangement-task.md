## Purpose and Scope

 This document describes the **Arrangement Task**, one of MAgent's built-in scenarios where agents learn to navigate to goal positions that form text patterns on the grid. This scenario demonstrates cooperative navigation, spatial reasoning, and multi-stage objectives with dynamic reward shaping.

 For general training workflows, see [Training Workflow](https://deepwiki.com/geek-ai/MAgent/4.1-training-workflow). For other built-in scenarios, see [Battle Scenario](https://deepwiki.com/geek-ai/MAgent/5.1-battle-scenario) and [Pursuit and Gather Scenarios](https://deepwiki.com/geek-ai/MAgent/5.2-pursuit-and-gather-scenarios).

 
## Task Overview

 The Arrangement Task teaches agents to form alphanumeric characters and messages by navigating to goal positions arranged in letter patterns. The task has two phases:

 
 - **Convergence Phase**: Agents navigate from the map perimeter to reach goal positions forming text patterns in the center
 - **Dispersion Phase**: After sufficient coverage, agents receive negative rewards for staying near the center, encouraging them to disperse
 
 The task can optionally include randomly-generated mazes that agents must navigate through to reach the goals.

 **Key Learning Objectives**:

 
 - Spatial navigation with limited view range (6 cells)
 - Long-horizon planning through maze corridors
 - Multi-stage behavior (approach then disperse)
 - Cooperative coverage of distributed goals

### Agent Placement Strategy

 **Goal Agents** (targets):

 
 - Form character patterns in the center region
 - Additional decorative goals placed in concentric squares around the center
 - Total goal count determined by character complexity
 
 **Mobile Agents** (learners):

 
 - Placed on 4 concentric squares near map perimeter (95%, 90%, 85%, 80% of map size)
 - Agent count is ~160% of goal count (overprovisioning)
 - Random sampling from perimeter positions to avoid clustering

### Dynamic Reward Modification

 The training implements a unique two-phase learning strategy:

 **Phase 1: Convergence (new_rule_ct == 0)**

 
 - Observation channels 10-11 set to 1 (signal to approach)
 - Standard collision rewards (+10)
 - Agents learn to navigate to goal positions
 
 **Phase 2: Dispersion (new_rule_ct > 0, triggered at 99% coverage)**

 
 - Observation channels 10-11 set to 0 (signal to disperse)
 - Additional distance-based penalty applied to rewards
 - Penalty proportional to proximity to center: `-0.5` to `-5.0`
 - Agents learn to move away from center after coverage

## Key Design Patterns

 
### Curriculum Learning

 The two-phase reward structure implements curriculum learning:

 
 - **Simple objective first**: Learn to reach static goals
 - **Complex objective second**: Learn to disperse while maintaining coverage
 
 
### Observation Augmentation

 Uses reserved observation channels (10-11) as explicit behavioral signals:

 
 - Eliminates need for the agent to infer task phase
 - Enables rapid policy switching between convergence/dispersion modes
 - More sample-efficient than learning phase detection
 
 
### Spatial Reasoning

 The task requires:

 
 - **Local navigation**: Avoid walls, navigate corridors
 - **Global planning**: Head toward center despite limited view range
 - **Memory**: Remember goal locations after absorption (when maze blocks view)

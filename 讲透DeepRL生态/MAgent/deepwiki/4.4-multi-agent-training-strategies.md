## Overview of Multi-Agent Training Paradigms

 MAgent supports three primary training paradigms:

 
| Paradigm | Description | Use Cases | Key Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| Competitive | Multiple models train against each other | Battle scenarios, adversarial games | examples/train_battle.py |
| Asymmetric | One model trains against a fixed opponent | Curriculum learning, benchmarking | examples/train_against.py |
| Cooperative | Multiple models work toward a common goal | Team coordination, multi-role tasks | examples/train_gather.py |

 The choice of paradigm depends on your research objectives and the structure of your environment.

 When training against opponents, gradually increasing opponent competence improves learning:

 
```

```

 For the first 100 rounds, the opponent uses the same exploration rate as the training model (high exploration). After round 100, the opponent becomes more deterministic (eps=0.05), providing consistent challenge.

### Progressive Opponent Switching

 Advanced curriculum strategies involve switching opponents as training progresses:

 
| Training Phase | Opponent Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Early (0-50 rounds) | RandomActor | Learn basic mechanics |
| Mid (50-200 rounds) | Weak pre-trained model | Learn tactics |
| Late (200+ rounds) | Strong pre-trained model | Refine strategy |

 This requires manually loading different opponent models at specified checkpoints.

### Exploration-Exploitation Balance

 General guidelines for epsilon scheduling:

 
 - **Early training (0-30% of rounds)**: High exploration (eps > 0.5)
 - **Mid training (30-70%)**: Moderate exploration (eps = 0.1-0.3)
 - **Late training (70-100%)**: Low exploration (eps = 0.05-0.1)
 
 Adjust based on environment complexity and convergence speed.

 
## Summary of Key Patterns

 
| Pattern | When to Use | Key Code Symbols |
|---|---|---|
| Symmetric Competition | Self-play-like scenarios with balance | leftID/rightID swap, parallel train() |
| Asymmetric Training | Curriculum learning, benchmarking | train_id, selective sample_step() |
| Non-blocking Inference | All multi-model scenarios | block=False, fetch_action() |
| Parallel Training | Competitive scenarios | block=False in train(), fetch_train() |
| Curriculum Scheduling | Improving convergence | piecewise_decay(), opponent switching |
| Step-based Training | On-policy methods (A2C) | step_batch_size, mid-episode train() |

 These patterns form the foundation for implementing sophisticated multi-agent training experiments in MAgent.

## Purpose and Overview

 The physics randomization system in KSIM provides a structured way to introduce variability in physical properties during reinforcement learning training. This domain randomization technique is crucial for developing robust policies that can generalize to real-world scenarios or handle model uncertainties. By randomizing physical parameters like friction, mass, and joint properties, agents learn to handle a wide range of environment variations rather than overfitting to specific simulation parameters.

 For information about the broader physics simulation system, see [Physics Simulation](https://deepwiki.com/kscalelabs/ksim/3-physics-simulation), and for details on the reset system that works alongside randomizers, see [Reset System](https://deepwiki.com/kscalelabs/ksim/3.3-reset-system).

## Available Randomizers

 KSIM provides several built-in randomizers for different physics properties:

 
| Randomizer | Purpose | Key Parameters | Affected Model Properties |
|---|---|---|---|
| StaticFrictionRandomizer | Randomizes joint static friction | scale_lower, scale_upper | dof_frictionloss |
| FloorFrictionRandomizer | Randomizes floor friction | floor_geom_id, scale_lower, scale_upper | geom_friction |
| ArmatureRandomizer | Randomizes joint armature (rotor inertia) | scale_lower, scale_upper | dof_armature |
| MassAdditionRandomizer | Adds random mass to a specific body | body_id, scale_lower, scale_upper | body_mass |
| MassMultiplicationRandomizer | Multiplies a body's mass by a random factor | body_id, scale_lower, scale_upper | body_mass |
| AllBodiesMassMultiplicationRandomizer | Randomizes mass of all bodies | scale_lower, scale_upper | body_mass |
| JointDampingRandomizer | Randomizes joint damping | scale_lower, scale_upper | dof_damping |
| JointZeroPositionRandomizer | Randomizes joint rest positions | scale_lower, scale_upper | qpos0 |

## Best Practices for Physics Randomization

 
### Recommended Randomization Ranges

 Here are typical ranges for different randomizers based on the default values in the code:

 
| Randomizer Type | Typical Lower Range | Typical Upper Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Static Friction | 0.5 | 2.0 | More variation is acceptable for friction |
| Floor Friction | 0.4 | 1.0 | Lower bound helps ensure enough friction for standing |
| Joint Damping | 0.9 | 1.1 | Small variations to avoid destabilizing motion |
| Armature | 0.95 | 1.05 | Small variations to maintain control stability |
| Body Mass | 0.98 | 1.02 | Small variations for all bodies, larger for targeted bodies |
| Joint Position | -0.01 | 0.01 | Very small variations to avoid breaking kinematic chains |

 
### Balancing Randomization

 When configuring randomizers, consider these principles:

 
 - **Start conservative**: Begin with narrow randomization ranges and gradually increase them.
 - **Prioritize physical properties**: Focus on randomizing properties that have the largest sim-to-real gaps.
 - **Balance stability and robustness**: Excessive randomization can make learning impossible while insufficient randomization won't develop robust policies.
 - **Layer randomizations**: Add randomizers one at a time to isolate their effects on training.

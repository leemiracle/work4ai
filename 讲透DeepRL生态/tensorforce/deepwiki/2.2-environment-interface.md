  The Environment Interface in Tensorforce provides a standardized way for agents to interact with environments in reinforcement learning. It abstracts the details of specific environment implementations (such as OpenAI Gym, custom environments, or third-party simulators) behind a consistent API, enabling the separation of reinforcement learning algorithms from the specific problems they solve.

 For information about how agents interact with environments, see [Agent Architecture](https://deepwiki.com/tensorforce/tensorforce/2.1-agent-architecture). For information about coordinating agent-environment interaction, see [Runner System](https://deepwiki.com/tensorforce/tensorforce/2.3-runner-system).

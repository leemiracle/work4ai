 - **TensorBoard**: Primary tool for real-time monitoring of training metrics and viewing videos
 - **Interactive Viewer**: For visualizing and testing trained policies with interactive controls
 - **K-Infer Simulator**: For visualizing converted models in a deployment-ready environment

### Interpreting TensorBoard Data

 When monitoring your training, pay attention to these key indicators:

 
 - **Total Reward**: Should increase over time, indicating policy improvement
 - **Curriculum Level**: Progression through levels (0-100) indicates the agent's mastery of the task
 - **Component Rewards**: Monitor individual reward components to identify specific areas of improvement: 
 - Forward progress reward
 - Upright orientation reward
 - Stay alive reward
 - Various penalties (joint acceleration, jerking, etc.)
 - **Videos**: Visual confirmation of the robot's behavior and stability

## Comparing Visualization Methods

 Each visualization method serves different purposes in the K-Sim Gym workflow:

 
| Tool | Purpose | Best Used For |
|---|---|---|
| TensorBoard | Training monitoring | Tracking metrics, watching progress videos, comparing runs |
| Interactive Viewer | Policy testing and debugging | Testing robustness by applying external forces, observing detailed behaviors |
| K-Infer Simulator | Deployment validation | Validating converted models before hardware deployment |

 
## Best Practices

 For effective monitoring and visualization:

 
 - **Regular TensorBoard Checking**: Monitor TensorBoard metrics every few minutes during early training
 - **Component Analysis**: Watch individual reward components to identify specific issues
 - **Curriculum Progression**: Ensure curriculum level increases steadily; stagnation may indicate learning problems
 - **Visual Validation**: Always validate metrics with visual observation of the policy behavior
 - **Force Testing**: Use the interactive viewer to apply forces to the robot to test its robustness
 - **Record Key Videos**: Save videos of significant breakthroughs or issues for documentation and comparison

## Purpose

 This document provides step-by-step instructions for submitting your trained humanoid robot controller to the K-Scale Leaderboard. The leaderboard showcases successfully trained models that have been tested on real robots and allows you to compare your approach with others in the community. For information about training your policy, see [Training a Policy](https://deepwiki.com/kscalelabs/ksim-gym/4.1-training-a-policy), and for converting models for deployment, see [Converting and Deploying Models](https://deepwiki.com/kscalelabs/ksim-gym/4.3-converting-and-deploying-models).

### 4. Submit Your Model

 The submission process involves:

 
 - Push your code, model, and video to your public GitHub repository
 - Join the [K-Scale Discord](https://url.kscale.dev/discord)
 - Post a message with a link to your repository in the "【🧠】submissions" channel
 - Wait for the K-Scale team to evaluate and test your model on a real robot
 
 After successful testing on a real robot, your submission will be added to the [leaderboard](https://kscale.dev/benchmarks).

 Sources: [README.md83-87](https://github.com/kscalelabs/ksim-gym/blob/3e92db5c/README.md?plain=1#L83-L87)

 
## Evaluation Process

 
```

```

 The K-Scale team will:

 
 - Review your code and model
 - Test your model in their simulation environment
 - Deploy your model on a real robot
 - Measure performance metrics such as walking distance and stability
 - Add your submission to the leaderboard with appropriate ranking
 
 Sources: [README.md85-87](https://github.com/kscalelabs/ksim-gym/blob/3e92db5c/README.md?plain=1#L85-L87)

 
## Leaderboard Ranking

 The leaderboard ranks submissions based on real-world performance metrics. While the exact evaluation criteria may evolve, they typically include:

 
 - Forward walking speed
 - Stability and robustness
 - Energy efficiency
 - Natural movement quality
 
 
## Tips for Successful Submissions

 
 - **Test thoroughly** before submission - ensure your model performs consistently in the simulator
 - **Keep your repository clean** and well-organized
 - **Document your approach** - describe any modifications or innovations
 - **Be patient** - testing on physical robots may take time
 - **Use Git LFS** for large model files
 
 Sources: [README.md82-87](https://github.com/kscalelabs/ksim-gym/blob/3e92db5c/README.md?plain=1#L82-L87)

 
## Troubleshooting

 If you encounter issues during the submission process:

 
 - Ensure your model file is properly committed (check Git LFS if needed)
 - Verify that your repository is public and accessible
 - Make sure your video demonstrates the performance clearly
 - Reach out on the [K-Scale Discord](https://url.kscale.dev/discord) if you don't receive feedback after a few days

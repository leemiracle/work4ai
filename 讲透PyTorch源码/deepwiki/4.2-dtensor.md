### Strategy Registration

- register_op_strategy — Registers a function (OpSchema) -> OpStrategy . OpStrategy holds a list of OpSpec objects, each describing one valid (input_placements, output_placements, redistribute_cost) tuple torch/distributed/tensor/_op_schema.py 15-25
- register_prop_rule — Registers a legacy function (OpSchema) -> OutputSharding . Used for older ops torch/distributed/tensor/_ops/utils.py 29-32
- register_single_dim_strategy — Registers sharding rules in terms of per-dimension placement patterns; these are expanded to full mesh strategies torch/distributed/tensor/_ops/single_dim_strategy.py 20-21

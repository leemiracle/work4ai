  This page documents the `IntegratedReportGenerator`, section-based synthesis, context accumulation, citation formatting, export formats, and the text optimization pipeline. The system is responsible for transforming raw research findings into structured, professional documents with accurate citations and multiple export options.

 
---

 
## Overview

 Report generation is the final stage of the research lifecycle. After an `AdvancedSearchSystem` has completed its iterative discovery phase, the findings are passed to the `IntegratedReportGenerator`. This component performs deep, section-specific research to build a comprehensive narrative. The resulting markdown is then processed by the `CitationFormatter` to apply specific hyperlink styles and finally passed to the export registry for conversion into formats like PDF, LaTeX, or Markdown.

## IntegratedReportGenerator

 The `IntegratedReportGenerator` (defined in `report_generator.py`) performs "Deep Research" by treating each section of a report as a standalone research task. It maintains a reference to an `AdvancedSearchSystem` to execute these tasks [src/local_deep_research/report_generator.py53-58](https://github.com/LearningCircuit/local-deep-research/blob/4a6a9088/src/local_deep_research/report_generator.py#L53-L58)

## Citation and Text Optimization

 The `CitationFormatter` handles the transition from plain-text citations (e.g., `[1]`) to interactive, hyperlinked references.

 
### Citation Modes

 The system supports several `CitationMode` [src/local_deep_research/text_optimization/citation_formatter.py32-43](https://github.com/LearningCircuit/local-deep-research/blob/4a6a9088/src/local_deep_research/text_optimization/citation_formatter.py#L32-L43) types:

 
 - `NUMBER_HYPERLINKS`: Standard `[[1]](url)` format.
 - `DOMAIN_HYPERLINKS`: Replaces numbers with source domains, e.g., `[[arxiv.org]](url)`.
 - `DOMAIN_ID_HYPERLINKS`: Uses smart IDs like `[[arxiv.org-1]]` if multiple sources share a domain.
 - `DOMAIN_ID_ALWAYS_HYPERLINKS`: Always appends IDs to domains.
 - `NO_HYPERLINKS`: Leaves citations as plain text.
 
 
### Implementation Details

 The formatter uses `find_sources_section` [src/local_deep_research/text_optimization/citation_formatter.py20-29](https://github.com/LearningCircuit/local-deep-research/blob/4a6a9088/src/local_deep_research/text_optimization/citation_formatter.py#L20-L29) to identify where the bibliography begins. It then parses the sources using `_parse_sources` [src/local_deep_research/text_optimization/citation_formatter.py173-201](https://github.com/LearningCircuit/local-deep-research/blob/4a6a9088/src/local_deep_research/text_optimization/citation_formatter.py#L173-L201) to map citation numbers to URLs. It specifically handles Unicode lenticular brackets `【】` often generated by LLMs [src/local_deep_research/text_optimization/citation_formatter.py52-55](https://github.com/LearningCircuit/local-deep-research/blob/4a6a9088/src/local_deep_research/text_optimization/citation_formatter.py#L52-L55)

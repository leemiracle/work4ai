  This document details the quantization strategies, specialized kernels, two-batch overlap mechanism, and optimization techniques tailored for Mixture of Experts (MoE) models in the SGLang codebase. MoE layers present unique challenges given their architecture with multiple experts and dynamic token routing; hence, dedicated quantization and execution optimizations are employed.

 For architecture and routing, refer to **8.1 MoE Layer Architecture** and **8.3 Expert Routing and Token Dispatch**. General quantization is covered in **9 Quantization System**.

## Quantization Methods Overview

 SGLang supports a suite of MoE-specific quantization formats optimized for different hardware, accuracy, and performance trade-offs.

 
| Format | Weight Precision | Activation Precision | Scale Granularity | Typical Hardware | Primary Usage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FP8 | float8_e4m3fn | float8_e4m3fn | Per-tensor, Block-wise | NVIDIA H100, AMD MI300 | General high throughput FP8 MoE |
| MXFP8 | float8_e4m3fn | float8_e4m3fn | Per-block (1x128) | NVIDIA Blackwell, ROCm gfx95 | Microscaling with block quant |
| NVFP4 | 4-bit FP4 | BF16 / FP8 | Per-block (1x32) | NVIDIA B200 / GB200 GPUs | Low-bit FP4 experts |
| W4AFp8 | 4-bit INT + FP8 | float8_e4m3fn | Mixed | General | Mixed INT4 weight with FP8 compute |
| INT8 | int8 | int8 | Per-channel | General | Standard INT8 quantization |

 This coverage integrates per-token-group (block) quantization to improve activation fidelity on routed tokens [python/sglang/srt/layers/quantization/fp8.py15-20](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/94183a8d/python/sglang/srt/layers/quantization/fp8.py#L15-L20)

## NVFP4 and MXFP4 Optimization

 SGLang integrates advanced 4-bit FP4 formats tailored for NVIDIA Blackwell and Hopper GPUs.

 
 - **NVFP4 Weight Padding:** The `pad_nvfp4_weight` function ensures that weights packed with 2 FP4 per byte align to kernel-required multiples (e.g., 32 or 128 columns/rows) to maximize kernel efficiency [python/sglang/srt/layers/quantization/modelopt_quant.py172-206](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/94183a8d/python/sglang/srt/layers/quantization/modelopt_quant.py#L172-L206)
 - **MXFP4 Interaction:** MXFP4 format uses block-scale packed FP8 weights. On AMD gfx95, `fused_rms_mxfp4_quant` is used for efficient normalization and quantization [python/sglang/srt/layers/communicator.py107-109](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/94183a8d/python/sglang/srt/layers/communicator.py#L107-L109)
 - **DeepSeek V4 FP4 Support:** Implements lossless casting for DeepSeek V4 FP4 experts from `e2m1fn` to `e4m3fn` format via `cast_e2m1fn_to_e4m3fn` [python/sglang/srt/layers/quantization/fp8.py180-220](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/94183a8d/python/sglang/srt/layers/quantization/fp8.py#L180-L220)
 - **Expert Parallelism Compatibility:** `DeepEPMoE` implementation handles varying quant configurations, including W4AFp8 and MXFP4, bridging block quantization with expert parallelism (EP) runtime dispatch [python/sglang/srt/layers/moe/ep_moe/layer.py154-178](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/94183a8d/python/sglang/srt/layers/moe/ep_moe/layer.py#L154-L178)

## Two-Batch Overlap (TBO) Optimization

 Two-batch overlap is an execution pattern that overlaps the MoE expert computation of one batch with other stages (such as attention or communication) of another batch to hide communication latency and improve throughput.

 Title: Two-Batch Overlap (TBO) Interaction
